# Privacy policy and terms: approved corrections

Approved by io under Q8 ("all approved. proceed."), 2026-10-13; fact-checked per P2-R24/P2-R28.

Both pages are dated November 6, 2025 and describe the old site. Below are the statements on both pages that are no longer true since the 2026-10-12 launch, or won't be once the features below turn on, with their replacements. Nothing here is live until it is published to both pages.

**Added for the family archive plan.** Rows 3.5 to 3.15 and the rows marked "addition" cover things that are not on yet: suggested changes, the inbox, moderation, contribution counts, photos added to family pages, profile links, joining, chat and online status. None of them turns on before this text is live. The three points the design had left unset (how long declined suggestions are kept, when photo files are cleaned up, and where Cloudflare keeps chat) are settled under your delegation by the architect's rulings P2-R24 and P2-R27 (recorded in the plan catalog, DOC-981BF41F2443), held to what the plan delivers: declined and taken-back suggestions are kept, as the amendment approved under Q8 says, until someone asks for one to be removed (3.6); a photo on a hidden post or comment is kept so it can be restored, and photo files are removed from storage on request (3.15); and this page names where Cloudflare keeps chat before chat is turned on (9).

If it's declined, you'll see why. Suggestions are kept after the decision too, including declined ones and ones you take back; you can ask for one to be removed (section 7). A suggestion gets the same check (3.5) before it's saved, and anything the check finds is refused. |
| 3.7 (new) Dates on family pages | (missing) | Birthdays show the year only, for everyone. If you edit a date that shows only its year, the day and month already stored stay as they were. "On this day" shows only to signed-in family members. It can show deaths, weddings and other events from today's date or this week; when there are none, round-number years from this year, worked out from the year alone (for a birth, "turns 40 this year" for a living relative, or "was born 100 years ago" for others); and family posts from this date in past years. It never shows the day or month of anyone's birth, or anything marked private. Anyone in an item, or an admin, can hide it. |
| 3.8 (new) Photos and location | (missing) | When you add a photo to a post or comment, your browser re-saves it before upload, which removes hidden details such as where it was taken. The server checks again and refuses any photo that still carries a location. Photos from the family tree that carry a location are held back until the location is removed. Photos added to posts before these checks were turned on were not re-checked and keep whatever details they were uploaded with, which can include where they were taken; ask (section 7) and we'll remove one. |
| 3.9 (new) Photos added to a family page | (missing) | An admin can add a photo from a comment to a person's photos on their family page. You're told when this happens to your photo, and it counts as one of your contributions. Photos that carry a location, or that a moderator has hidden, can't be added. |
| 3.10 (new) Contribution counts | (missing) | The site counts what you add. Each post, comment, photo, approved suggestion, photo tag and photo added to a family page counts as one, and so does editing your own page (at most once per page per day). Your total shows on your page, including "0 contributions". A line for the whole family, such as "12 contributions in the past 3 days", shows on the home page and the posts page. Edits to posts or comments, reactions, moderation and approvals don't count, and deleting something removes its count. Only signed-in family members see counts. |
| 3.11 (new) Profile links | (missing) | You can add links to your public profiles on Facebook, Instagram, Snapchat, TikTok, Linktree and LinkedIn, and to one website. They show in a "links" section on your family page, to signed-in family members. Only you can add or change them; an admin can remove one. Links that are a way to message you (email, phone, text, WhatsApp, Messenger and the like), short links and share links are refused. Links stay on the website only (they're not copied into the family tree) and are kept until you remove them. |
| 3.12 (new) Asking to join | (missing) | On the join page you give your email and prove it's yours with a 6-digit code, then fill in the family form: your names, gender (male or female), where you were born and the year (never the full birthday), where you live now (city and state only), and optional details: what the family calls you, the year you moved to where you live now, school details, and whether you were ever married (that answer helps the admin and isn't added to the family tree). Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you're a person and not a bot; it loads on the join page only. An admin checks with you outside the site before approving, and sees only part of your email. If you're new to the family tree, you get a permanent family-tree number from a block kept for the website (8000-8999). We keep the code for 15 minutes; the one-time pass it gives you for 60 minutes or until it's used; a request with no decision for 90 days; and a decided request for 30 days after the decision. A scrambled copy of your email that stops duplicate requests is kept as long as the request, and for 30 days after a decline. After that, only a note of the request's number and outcome remains. Your email stays on the family access list while you have access, and never goes into the family tree, onto a page or into a log. |
| 3.13 (new) Chat | (missing) | Chat messages and group names are stored by Cloudflare, which runs chat for us as a processor (it handles them only to run the site's chat), with a copy in our own AWS storage. You can see the last 30 days of a chat; older messages disappear from every screen. The site keeps messages with no end date, and older ones can be pulled out only with the admin's go-ahead, as a one-off export from storage. If you leave a group and come back, you see only what was said while you were in it. Chat is text and emoji only: no photos, reactions or read receipts, and no "last seen". While you type, the other person can see that you're typing. Chat notifications never include the message. Chat text gets the same check as posts (3.5). |
| 3.14 (new) Online status and accounts | (missing) | Online status: while you have the site open, every family member can see that you are online or idle, on your page and in chat; there is no invisible mode. "Online" means the site is open on any of your devices, plus 20 seconds after you close it. You show as idle after 15 minutes with no typing, tapping or scrolling, and idle still counts as online. A computer keeps you online while the site is open, even in a background tab; a phone goes offline about 20 seconds after it locks or you switch apps. Only your current status is kept, never a history. Chat lists every relative who has an account (by name, never by email), so family members can see who has joined. |
